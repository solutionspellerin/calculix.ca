About Calculix.ca

Calculix.ca brings together free financial calculators built for Québec: tips, budgets, investments, NPV and IRR, loan amortization, pay equity and invoices with GST and QST.

Who is behind Calculix.ca

Calculix.ca is designed and maintained by Marie-Pier Pellerin, founder of Solutions Pellerin, a company based in Val-des-Sources (Québec) that specializes in process optimization and business intelligence. Her work involves helping organizations automate their processes and build dashboards (Zoho, Power BI, Excel), and designing training, including Lean Six Sigma, Excel and Power BI courses.

Calculix.ca applies the same approach to everyday questions: start from a precise rule — a discounting formula, an amortization schedule, an expense split, the taxes on an invoice — and turn it into a calculation that is clear, verifiable and free.

Our tools

  • 💵 Tip splitting

    Equal shares or in proportion to hours worked, with Excel export.

  • 📈 Investment projection

    Time or amount needed to reach a goal, with compound returns and contributions.

  • 📊 NPV and IRR

    Net present value, internal rate of return and payback period of a project.

  • 🏠 Amortization

    Loan or mortgage payments and the principal/interest split, period by period.

  • 💑 Couple budget

    Sharing joint expenses in proportion to income or by a chosen percentage.

  • 👤 Personal budget

    Income, expenses, savings rate and the 50/30/20 rule.

  • ⚖️ Pay equity

    Job class evaluation with weighted factors, based on Québec's Pay Equity Act.

  • 🧾 Invoices and receipts

    Invoices with GST/QST, HST or no tax, history and printable PDF.

How our calculations are built

  • Every calculator is based on a standard formula (compound interest, discounting, amortization) or a published rule, explained below the tool.
  • Calculations run entirely in your browser: the amounts and details you enter are neither stored nor transmitted by our calculators.
  • Assumptions are shown (constant rate, compounding frequency, rounding) so that you can redo the calculation yourself.
  • Results are estimates: a real investment varies with markets, and your financial institution may round or calculate differently.

Updates and corrections

Rules change: public plan parameters, rates, public holidays, laws. We review the tools when those rules change. If you spot an error or a case that is handled poorly, write to info@calculix.ca with the values you entered and the result you expected: every report is checked and corrected where needed.
